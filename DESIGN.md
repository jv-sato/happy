## Brand & Style

This design system expresses celebration, optimism, and frictionless momentum. Designed for an event planning marketplace and SaaS platform, it channels the thrill of hosting memorable parties while delivering clear, reliable utility.

### Brand Personality
- **Festive & Vibrant:** Radiates celebration without veering into chaos. Employs crisp contrasts and punchy bursts of color.
- **Approachable & Welcoming:** Friendly, rounded geometry puts users immediately at ease during inherently stressful planning workflows.
- **Enthusiastic & Confident:** Balances playful celebration with structured reliability, guiding hosts and vendors through seamless bookings.
- **Uncomplicated:** Replaces clutter with spacious, digestible cards, direct calls to action, and swift visual feedback.

### Visual Style
A blend of modern geometric warmth and punchy celebration design:
- Clean, open canvases bathed in soft porcelain-white, grounded by electric deep purple text and borders.
- Radiant celebratory accents driven by a sunshine yellow core, accented with purple-tinted depth.
- Generous, organic corner curves (16px to 24px) paired with pill-shaped chips and tactile, clickable buttons.
- Micro-delights: crisp bouncy states, confetti micro-accents, and buoyant card elevations.

## Colors

The palette pairs high-energy celebration tones with crisp accessibility and strong visual hierarchy.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#FFDD44` - Celebration Yellow):** Powers high-priority interactive touchpoints, primary CTAs, highlight badges, active selections, and joyful achievement states. Always paired with `#1A103D` for readable text contrast.
- **Secondary (`#581CFF` - Electric Violet):** Commands authority, brand anchors, interactive links, deep button fills, headers, and focus halos.
- **Tertiary (`#22C55E` - Confetti Green):** Signals success, confirmation, finalized vendor contracts, and confirmed RSVPs.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1A103D` - Deep Plum Midnight):** High-contrast base for primary typography, body text, and line icons, ensuring much softer harmony with the violet palette than pure carbon black.
- **Backgrounds & Canvases:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8F9FA` (Clean Cloud).
  - Surface cards & overlays: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
  - Subtle borders: `#ECE8FD` (Gentle Violet Tint) and `#E2E8F0` (Neutral Whisper).

## Typography

This design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to maintain a cohesive, joyful geometry with open counters and balanced round letterforms.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Extra Bold (`800`) and Bold (`700`) weights with tight negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) for a confident, welcoming editorial presence.
- **Body:** Uses Normal (`400`) and Medium (`500`) with generous line-height ratios (`1.5` to `1.55`) to keep marketplace descriptions, vendor invoices, and guest checklists easy to scan.
- **Labels & Badges:** Rendered in Semi-Bold (`600`) and Bold (`700`) with slightly loose tracking to ensure legibility on miniature event tags and interactive controls.

## Layout & Spacing

A unified 8pt grid with 4pt baseline intervals ensures reliable spatial harmony across web, tablet, and mobile surfaces.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid, `2.5rem` section margin, `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers capped at max-width `1280px` for optimal reading flow and card balance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `1.75rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Multi-column vendor cards collapse into 2-column tiles.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` canvas margins, `1rem` column gutters. Cards span full width or sit in horizontal carousels with edge bleeding.

### Layout Principles
- Use generous white space between modules (`space-xl`) to counteract event planning cognitive overload.
- Dense data chunks (guest seatings, budget tallies, checklists) stay organized using tight `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps inside dedicated white surface cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system blends flat, friendly modernism with ambient violet diffusion. Instead of cold industrial gray drop shadows, elevation relies on warm, tinted shadows that echo the electric purple brand palette.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas (`#F8F9FA`) and embedded surface panels. Outlined with soft borders (`1px solid #ECE8FD`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Tiles):** Elevated white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) floating over the canvas.
  - Shadow: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(88, 28, 255, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 16, 61, 0.04)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(88, 28, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive Cards):** Applied when hovering over vendor listings or interactive widgets.
  - Shadow: `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(88, 28, 255, 0.12), 0px 4px 10px -2px rgba(26, 16, 61, 0.04)`.
  - Transform: `translateY(-3px)` with standard spring transition (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-overs & Sticky Navigation):** High visual priority overlay surfaces.
  - Shadow: `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(26, 16, 61, 0.18), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(88, 28, 255, 0.10)`.
  - Backdrop blur: `8px` with semi-opaque Plum-tinted overlay (`rgba(26, 16, 61, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The shape system prioritizes generous, friendly curves that convey warmth and ease.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Small Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Chips):** Radius `12px` to `9999px` (full pill shape for tags, chips, and standard CTAs).
- **Cards & Content Containers:** Default radius `16px` to `20px` (`rounded-2xl`). This softens standard grid lines and produces comfortable visual grouping.
- **Large Modals & Feature Banners:** Radius `24px` (`rounded-3xl`), anchoring focal points with friendly curves.
- **Avatars & Celebration Stamps:** Standard circular radius (`9999px`) or organic rounded squares (`16px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Celebration Button:**
  - Background: Primary Yellow (`#FFDD44`).
  - Text: Deep Plum Midnight (`#1A103D`), weight `700`.
  - Shape: Rounded pill (`9999px`) or `14px` border-radius.
  - Hover: Background `#FFE566`, soft elevation shadow `0px 6px 16px rgba(255, 221, 68, 0.45)`, gentle scale `1.02`.
  - Active: Scale `0.98`.
- **Secondary Electric Button:**
  - Background: Electric Violet (`#581CFF`).
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, weight `600`.
  - Hover: Background `#4814DB`, shadow `0px 6px 18px rgba(88, 28, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:**
  - Background: Transparent or `#F3F0FF`.
  - Text: `#581CFF`, weight `600`.
  - Hover: Background `#ECE8FD`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Fully rounded pills (`padding: 4px 12px`).
  - Active/Featured: Background `#FFF8D6`, text `#8A6700`, bold label.
  - Confirmed: Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`.
  - Category Filter Chip: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #ECE8FD`, text `#1A103D`. When selected, background transitions to `#581CFF` with white text.

### Cards (Vendor, Event & Budget)
- **Base Style:** Solid `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `20px`, Level 1 ambient violet shadow, `1px solid #ECE8FD`.
- **Header Asset:** Aspect ratio `16:9` with `16px` inner radius or top-clipping matching the parent container.
- **Interactive State:** Hover elevates to Level 2 shadow with a `-3px` upward translation and subtle border tint toward Violet (`#581CFF`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F9FA`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `14px`, height `48px`.
- **Text:** Font size `16px`, color `#1A103D`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#581CFF`, focus ring `0 0 0 4px rgba(88, 28, 255, 0.15)`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Placeholder:** `#94A3B8`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** `20px x 20px`, corner radius `6px`. Inactive border `2px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state fills `#581CFF` with a crisp white check icon.
- **Radio Button:** `20px x 20px`, fully circular. Checked state features a `#581CFF` ring with an inner `#FFDD44` or `#581CFF` dot.

### Specialized Party/Event Components
- **Countdown Hero Tile:** White card housing large display numerals in Electric Violet (`#581CFF`) paired with celebratory yellow confetti icons and pill badges.
- **RSVP Progress Bar:** Track background `#ECE8FD`, fill bar `#FFDD44` with a smooth right cap, height `10px`, fully rounded.
- **Budget Tracker Donut/Card:** Neutral cards highlighting spent and remaining amounts, paired with Electric Violet and Confetti Green status indicators.
